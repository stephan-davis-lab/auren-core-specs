# Code of Conduct — AUREN Core Specs

**Adopted by the maintainer for the v2.5 release · 2026-10-10**

AUREN Core Specs welcomes careful criticism, different perspectives and concrete counterexamples. People should be able to question the specification and its implementation without being personally attacked or pressured to disclose private experiences.

## Scope

This policy covers this repository's issues, pull requests and other project-managed discussion spaces. It also covers conduct when someone explicitly represents this project. It does not appoint the maintainer as an authority over unrelated conversations or communities.

## Working together

- Discuss rules, evidence and consequences. Explain disagreements with references or examples.
- Distinguish a specification requirement from an implementation choice and from an observed model response.
- Accept corrections and make uncertainty visible. A review result is open to challenge.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, abilities and levels of experience.
- Respect a person's choice to stop a discussion or decline personal reflection.
- Share only material you have the right to disclose. Use synthetic examples or carefully redacted excerpts.

Criticism of AUREN, its author, its review methods or a proposed release is welcome when expressed without harassment. Agreement with the framework is not a condition for reporting a problem.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, targeted humiliation and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. This also includes publishing someone's private information without permission, using psychological labels or presumed motives to discredit a contributor, and deliberately exposing credentials or private conversations.

An incorrect claim, a failed test or a firmly expressed disagreement does not by itself establish misconduct. Moderation should address the conduct and its context rather than infer someone's inner state or intentions.

## Reporting a concern

The maintainer is **Stephan A. Davis**. Send conduct concerns privately to [stephan@stephanadavis.de](mailto:stephan@stephanadavis.de), with a subject such as `AUREN Core — conduct concern`.

Include the relevant project location, what happened, a short necessary excerpt and how you can be contacted. Avoid sending unrelated personal information or complete private chat histories. If the concern involves the maintainer, say so directly; this project does not currently provide a separate independent appeals body. GitHub's own reporting mechanisms remain available for conduct on its platform.

Reports should be handled discreetly. Only information needed to assess and address the concern should be shared. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised when action or platform reporting requires disclosure; those limits should be explained where possible.

## Moderation

Depending on severity and context, the maintainer may request a correction, give a warning, remove or lock content, or restrict participation in project-managed spaces. Where practicable, the affected person should receive the reason and an opportunity to respond. Serious or repeated misconduct may require immediate action.

Moderation decisions concern participation in these spaces. They do not revoke permissions granted by the repository's content licenses.
